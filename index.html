Allow Pop-ups in Chrome

Google Chrome by default blocks websites from opening multiple tabs at once to protect against spam pop-ups.

1 Look at your Chrome address bar (URL bar).

Click the small "Pop-up blocked" icon (🚫) on the far right of the address bar.

2 Select "Always allow pop-ups and redirects from this site".

3 Click Done, then click "Open All Tabs" again!
